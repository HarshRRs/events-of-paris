Privacy
information.

This page describes the current preview website. A complete privacy policy needs organiser confirmation before mailing-list signup or ticket sales are activated.

PREVIEW STATUS The email form does not create a subscription. Tickets are not sold on this website.

Email announcements

The homepage currently shows a demonstration email form. Its script prevents submission and displays a message that no subscription was made. The address entered is not sent by that form to Desi Events or saved by the page.

The mailing-list provider, consent text, retention period and contact for privacy requests have not been confirmed. Signup will remain a preview until those details are provided.

Tickets

This website does not currently offer checkout or collect payment details. When ticket sales open, the event page will identify the booking destination and link to its applicable privacy information.

Website services

The site is deployed on Vercel and loads selected interface libraries from jsDelivr. Visiting the site may involve requests to those services. The organiser still needs to confirm the full list of service providers and the final legal wording.

Information still needed

  • Organiser legal identity and privacy contact.
  • Confirmed hosting and analytics configuration, if any.
  • Mailing-list provider, lawful basis, retention and unsubscribe process.
  • Ticket provider and its privacy terms when sales begin.

This page will be updated before those features launch. For now, please use the site to browse the event previews only.
